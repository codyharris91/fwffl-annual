Chapter Three

The Median Game

Since 2022 the league plays twice a week: your opponent, and the league median. It is the format's best idea, because it puts a floor under bad luck — you can lose to the highest score of the week and still bank a win for being good. Everything here is checked against Sleeper's own result strings, which record both games.

Method

Built from the Sleeper API: 972 team-weeks, 16048 rostered player-weeks, 858 draft picks and 2019 transactions across 5 completed seasons. Every player is scored under this league's own settings (0.5 PPR, 0.5 TE bonus, 5.0-point passing touchdowns, 0.5 per first down), which reproduces Sleeper's published totals exactly — so players no team ever rostered can be scored too.

Head-to-head records exclude the weekly median game; official standings include it. Rate statistics use regular-season weeks only, because Sleeper still scores eliminated teams during the playoffs. Draft value compares each pick to its own position at its own slot.

Not available anywhere in the API, and therefore not here: league chat, and per-pick draft timestamps.
